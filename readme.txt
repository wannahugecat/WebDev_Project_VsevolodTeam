Project Description: Wheels on Fire

Overview:

A simple, accessible website for Wheels on Fire,
showcasing John O’Reilly’s personal car collection and offering expert buying advice with his signature dry humor.
Key Features:

* Car Collection: Interactive display of featured vehicles and specs.
* John’s Reviews: Unfiltered advice and opinions on car models and tuning.
* Accessibility First: Integrated disability friendly contrast and navigation features.
Goal:
Connect car enthusiasts and serious buyers directly with a perfectionist’s handpicked inventory, 
and honest guidance.
