ABOUT JOHN AND THE COLLECTION

A collector. An occasional optimist.

John O’Reilly’s Cars is a personal collection, plainly presented. Useful modern classics, sentimental favourites and the odd car that makes absolutely no practical sense.

The common thread isn’t a badge or a decade. It’s a car you’d want to drive, spend time with and, quite possibly, keep.

Some cars make a perfectly ordinary road feel like a good idea.

THE COLLECTING PHILOSOPHY

Head first. Heart very much involved.

“Buy the best car you can afford, not the fastest badge you can explain to your bank manager. And if the roof leaks, call it ventilation.”

— John O’Reilly, collector and occasional optimist

WHAT BELONGS HERE

Some sensible. Some sentimental.

There’s room for a useful modern classic and a car whose main qualification is making you smile. They don’t have to agree with one another. It’s a collection, not a committee.

Some sensible, some sentimental, all here for a reason. That’s about as tidy as the definition needs to be.

HOW CARS ARE CHOSEN

Hand picked. Not picked by a spreadsheet.

No single formula. Just a few things worth keeping in mind before enthusiasm takes the keys.

The car, not just the badge

A famous name is a starting point, not an argument. The particular car matters more than the reputation on its bonnet.

Something worth driving

Useful, entertaining or simply full of character. There should be a reason to take the long way home, even if the short way would do.

A reason to keep it

Not every choice needs to be sensible. But it should make sense to the person who wants it. Sentiment is allowed a seat at the table.

ENOUGH ABOUT JOHN

The cars are better company.

Have a look around. Start with the recommendations, or wander through the collection. There’s no need to be sensible straight away.
